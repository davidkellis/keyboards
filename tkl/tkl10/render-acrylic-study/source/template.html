Sources & fit notes

The acrylic is shown inside the opaque printed surround, with a removable white backing. Its visual thickness is the existing 1.5 mm CAD envelope. Kailh's drawing references 1.2 mm aluminum; actual acrylic thickness and Whale clip fit are not yet specified.

The emission maps use six normalized source kernels. They illustrate concentrated, scattered and patterned extraction, not measured PMMA properties or a ray trace. Engraved acrylic needs effective entry coupling to achieve the intended appearance. Expect real hot spots and dark regions.

ScottoCaps Flat and Glass MX meshes by Joe Scotto, used under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. XVX shapes and seating are approximate. The raised case targets nominal XVX skirts; other caps sit differently.

The 5000 mAh battery reserve, board and key positions stay fixed. A separate acrylic plate needs a new retention design; this study does not release print or laser-cut manufacturing files.
