One factory assembly side

Keep every factory-installed component on the bottom. Side-emitting LEDs shine parallel to the PCB into the guide below it. The driver, inductor, diodes and passives mount on that same side.

The guide, reflector and mask are fitted after soldering. You still install the switches and controller module. Use one LED part number and common passives where suitable.

JLC Economic supports single-side assembly. Preserve lead-free HASL and lead-free solder; check the exact parts against Economic requirements before committing.

Choose the driver around assembly cost

The earlier TPS61162A candidate uses a 0.4 mm BGA pitch, below JLC’s published 0.5 mm Economic BGA limit. It is excluded from the low-cost baseline.

For ten LEDs, evaluate one series string with a leaded driver such as TPS61169 in SC70. The ten sources can still occupy two physical edge banks. For twelve, evaluate two regulated six-LED strings with suitable packages; do not assume twelve white LEDs fit the same single-string voltage limit.

Preserve the existing PWM control signal. Low-current efficiency, exact forward-voltage margin, component stock and the complete assembly quote still need qualification. The circuit and footprints are not finalized.

40 mW is a low-power design target, not a promise of readable legends. More sources at the same input power means less current per source; runtime stays the same. The prototype must establish the least power that produces acceptable lighting.

What the MX Keys example tells us

A separate guide is visible in the MX Keys disassembly. A small number of sources is a useful architecture to pursue.

Logitech also documents automatic brightness, proximity wake and a low-battery cutoff. Those controls help runtime alongside the guide.

The exact ten-source count for the referenced revision is not independently confirmed here. Our mechanical switches need their own tested optical path.

Prototype a full-depth strip first

The 70.152 × 129.19 mm coupon has eighteen outputs, six physical rows and two sources. Its transport distance matches the keyboard’s full depth. It screens the harder case of nine outputs per source.

  1. Align a real Whale switch and XVX cap over an output; confirm the light passage’s orientation.
  2. Compare sparse, medium and dense extraction, first solid and then perforated. Use identical current and stock.
  3. Measure near, middle and far legends at fixed exposure. Aim initially for the darkest comparable legend to reach at least half the brightest.
  4. Use about one fifth of full ten-source LED output power. Test case fit, actual component reliefs and the full keyboard before routing a production board.

The generic pin reliefs omit real diode and mounting-boss cutouts. The guide is not a load-bearing plate or a drop-in V10 part. Bench brightness is not yet a full-board result.
