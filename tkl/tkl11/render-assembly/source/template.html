Sources & fit notes

The acrylic is shown inside the opaque printed surround, with a removable white backing. Its visual thickness is the existing 1.5 mm CAD envelope. Kailh's drawing references 1.2 mm aluminum; actual acrylic thickness and Whale clip fit are not yet specified.

The emission maps use ten normalized source kernels. They illustrate concentrated, scattered and patterned extraction, not measured PMMA properties or a ray trace. Engraved acrylic needs effective entry coupling to achieve the intended appearance. Expect real hot spots and dark regions.

ScottoCaps Flat and Glass MX meshes by Joe Scotto, used under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. XVX shapes and seating are approximate. The raised case targets nominal XVX skirts; other caps sit differently.

At zero separation, the nominal battery, real V11 PCB and all 86 switch centers use the existing assembly coordinates. The V10 upper integrates the switch plate; the acrylic view partitions it into a frame and separate plate as a fit concept. Exploding these layers illustrates their relationship; soldered switches cannot be lifted from a soldered PCB this way in servicing.
