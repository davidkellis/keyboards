# v9.2 — plain switch plate inside a continuous case rim

This mechanical revision replaces v9.1's integrated plate/top frame with a
separate, plain switch plate. The bottom case walls extend to the original top
height, so the outside walls have no horizontal lid joint. The plate sits about
1.6 mm below the rim. There is a fitting gap around the plate on the inside;
the assembly is removable, not a seamless bonded shell.

The v9.1 PCB, circuitry, switch positions, twelve mounting holes and M2 × 5 mm
screw/DIN 934 nut stack are unchanged. The footprint remains 355.56 × 133.352 mm
and the outside height remains 7.3 mm at the front / 20.6 mm at the rear.
v9.1 and all earlier revisions are preserved.

Print the new rim and rear-corner samples before committing to the full case.
The removed frame contributed stiffness; the existing distributed screws, PCB
supports and floor ribs remain, but the new printed plate needs a physical
flex/rattle test. CAD checks do not establish printed strength or switch,
stabilizer, keycap, connector or electrical operation.
